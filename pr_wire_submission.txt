-----------------------------------------------------------------------

New Free Tool Lets Any Texas Consumer Check Contractor Bond Status
Before Hiring; 29.3% of All TDLR Licensees Found Non-Compliant

DALLAS, TX, May 27, 2026 — A new analysis of Texas Department of
Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) public records reveals that 46.9
percent of Texas apprentice electricians — 93,509 out of 199,435 —
currently have expired surety bonds, technically violating state
licensing requirements.

The finding comes from a comprehensive database built by Quantum Surety
LLC, a Texas-licensed surety bond agency (TDI License #3480229), which
has indexed all 816,000+ TDLR contractor license records into a free,
publicly searchable compliance tracker at quantumsurety.bond/texas-bond-
watch.

KEY FINDINGS (from TDLR public records):
• 46.9% of Texas apprentice electricians: expired surety bonds
• 44.8% of consent tow truck operators: expired bonds
• 38.9% of Texas A/C technicians: expired bonds
• 29.3% of ALL TDLR licensees statewide: non-compliant

"A surety bond is a consumer protection mechanism — it's what a
homeowner can make a claim against when a licensed contractor causes
damage and can't or won't make it right," said Theodore Sparks, founder
of Quantum Surety LLC. "When nearly 100,000 electricians are operating
with lapsed bonds, consumers who hire them in good faith have materially
reduced legal protection, even though those contractors still have
license numbers."

THE FREE TOOL
Any Texas consumer, property manager, real estate agent, or title
company can now verify any TDLR-licensed contractor's bond status in
real time at quantumsurety.bond/texas-bond-watch. The site covers all
816,000+ TDLR records and 558,000+ Texas notary commissions, updated
daily from public records.

A trade-by-trade compliance grade report (A through F) is available at
quantumsurety.bond/bond-compliance-by-trade, showing all 22 TDLR
license categories ranked by expiration rate.

Quantum Surety also offers instant online bond renewals for out-of-
compliance contractors and notaries at quantumsurety.bond, starting at
$50 per year.
