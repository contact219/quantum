## 2. What We Do
Quantum Surety is an **AI-powered surety bond agency**. The company specializes in issuing a wide variety of surety bonds required by states, municipalities, and federal agencies. Rather than issuing bonds on their own paper, they act as a licensed insurance producer, placing bonds through a network of appointed carrier partners. 

They provide bonds for individuals and businesses, focusing heavily on contractors, notaries, and auto dealers.

## 3. How We Operate
Quantum Surety differentiates itself through a seamless, technology-driven process:
* **Speed & Convenience:** They offer instant approvals and immediate PDF delivery for many standard bonds (such as Texas Notary and Contractor License bonds). For larger, more complex bonds (like performance and payment bonds), they typically provide same-day or 24-hour turnaround for qualified applicants.
* **AI & Online Tools:** The ecosystem is built around user-friendly online tools designed to simplify the bond process. These include an "AI Bond Finder," a quote wizard, and a bond/license verification tool.
* **No Hidden Fees:** They offer flat-rate pricing for standard bonds, such as a $50 flat fee for a 4-year Texas Notary Bond without a credit check.

## 4. Ecosystem & Key Products
The Quantum Surety ecosystem offers a broad range of bonding solutions and resources to support its clients:

### Primary Bond Products
* **Texas Notary Bonds:** $10,000 bond for a 4-year term at a flat $50 rate. Fully compliant with Texas SB693 regulations.
* **Contractor License Bonds:** Required for TDLR-licensed trades (HVAC, electrical, plumbing) and many Texas cities.
* **Texas GDN Auto Dealer Bonds:** $50,000 bond required by TxDMV for all licensed auto dealers.
* **Construction Bonds (Contract Surety):**
  * **Bid Bonds:** Guaranteeing a contractor will honor their bid on public projects.
  * **Performance Bonds:** Guaranteeing project completion (required on Texas public contracts over $25,000).
  * **Payment Bonds:** Protecting subcontractors and suppliers from non-payment.
* **Texas Mortgage Broker Bonds:** Required by TDSML for licensed Texas mortgage companies.
* **Commercial Surety Bonds:** License and permit bonds for various regulated businesses.
