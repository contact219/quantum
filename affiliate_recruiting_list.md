## Recommended approach
This is a small, relationship-driven world — only 6 of 10 targets have a public email.
Treat this as **personal outreach, not a blast**: Ted should personally email/call Tier 1
(especially Phyllis Traylor and Liz Head, who run recurring live classes full of
brand-new notaries needing a bond within days) rather than running it through the
automated campaign scripts. Tier 2/3 need a form submission or DM first to get an email
address before any templated send makes sense.
